# Logos

- `capyfolio-lockup.png` — Full logo: the capybara in a graduation cap holding a green folder, above the "CapyFolio" wordmark with a cap on the C. Raster on its original textured off-white ground; use on `paper` or white only. Splash, print, the about page.
- `capyfolio-wordmark.png` — The wordmark alone ("Capy" navy, "y" green, "Folio" yellow, cap on the C). Raster on textured off-white; use on `paper` or white only. Note the logo's yellow (about #F8B800) is lighter than the UI's `gold` (#FEA402); in the product UI set the wordmark as live text instead.
- `capy-mascot.png` — The mascot alone on a transparent background (556×625). Used in the nav (36×41px), the login hero and the MascotDisc. Its dark outline vanishes on navy: always put it on a `paper` disc there. Edges were cut from the logo with a threshold and may be slightly rough; replace with an original transparent export when one exists.
